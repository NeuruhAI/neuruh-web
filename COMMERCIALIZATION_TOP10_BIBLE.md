# NEURUH TOP 10 PRODUCT CODEX

**Purpose:** convert existing Neuruh engineering, intelligence, verification, and commercialization assets into ten explicit products with a single repeatable execution pattern.

**Commercial law:** PRODUCT EXISTS → OFFER EXISTS → CHECKOUT / QUOTE EXISTS → INTAKE EXISTS → FULFILLMENT EXISTS → DELIVERY EXISTS → RECEIPT EXISTS → OUTCOME EXISTS → REPEAT REVENUE EXISTS.

Do not call a product “live” because code exists. The product is live only at the rung actually proven.

## Common execution spine

1. Pick one buyer and one painful decision.
2. Bind the requested outcome to an explicit scope.
3. Collect only the evidence needed for that outcome.
4. Run the existing governed capability. Do not invent a new architecture.
5. Record missing evidence and uncertainty instead of filling gaps.
6. Deliver a human-readable artifact plus a machine-readable receipt where applicable.
7. Ask for the outcome: did the buyer act, save money, close a deal, reduce risk, or approve a release?
8. Convert the outcome into the next offer.

## Product 01 — Agent Integrity Suite

**Buyer:** AI startups, agencies, internal AI teams, PE operating companies.

**Problem:** an agent can appear to work while nobody can prove what it actually did, what it was allowed to do, whether it regressed, or whether its evidence is trustworthy.

**Offer:** a fixed-scope assurance pilot against one workflow. Baseline the workflow, define allowed actions, run golden/held-out/adversarial cases, inspect evidence, and issue a promotion/acceptance receipt.

**Price:** start at $2,500 for a pilot. Target $1,500–$5,000/mo for recurring assurance after proof. These are Neuruh test prices, not market claims.

**Delivery:** workflow map, authority boundary, baseline, test corpus, failure report, release/acceptance receipt, remediation queue.

**Close condition:** customer can answer “promote, shadow, repair, or reject?” from the receipt.

**Existing substrate:** `neuruh-verify`, `neuruh-factory`, `neuruh-sentinel-court`, `neuruh-axon`, `neuruh-sops`, `neuruh-live-integration`, `crucible`, `ncell`, cockpit ratification work.

**Do not build:** another general agent framework. Productize the assurance substrate already built.

## Product 02 — AI Estate Forensic

**Buyer:** founders, PE technical diligence teams, companies with inherited AI automation, agencies with agent sprawl.

**Problem:** expensive AI estates contain stale branches, duplicated capability, silent authority assumptions, abandoned work, and “working” claims with no proof.

**Offer:** a fixed-scope estate inventory plus recovery map.

**Price:** $2,500–$7,500 per audit. Escalate to implementation only after the recovery map is accepted.

**Delivery:** repository census, branch/lineage map, harness inventory, runtime/authority map, canonical-vs-historical separation, stranded-assets register, ship/kill/hold queue, commercialization map.

**Close condition:** the customer knows what to keep, what to kill, and what to ship next.

**Existing substrate:** Neuruh topology courts, convergence handoffs, recovery ledgers, factory manifests, public-truth membrane, receipts.

## Product 05 — Business Acquisition Intelligence

**Buyer:** searchers, independent sponsors, small-business buyers, family offices, investors.

**Problem:** small-business listings hide owner transition, property, operational, and public-signal opportunities that are invisible in normal screening.

**Offer:** narrow-vertical, narrow-geography buyability screen followed by a dossier.

**Test price:** $750 screen, $2,500 dossier, custom buy-side mandate.

**Delivery:** target shortlist, buyability score, owner/succession signals, business-property linkage, risk/unknown ledger, diligence queue.

**Close condition:** buyer has 3–10 targets worth calling or diligencing.

**Existing substrate:** business acquisition lineage, buyability concepts, `acquire.html`, owner/entity resolution, transition intelligence recipes.

**Do not build first:** a giant marketplace. Sell the screen manually and instrument outcomes.

## Product 10 — FindSellProfit

**Buyer:** resellers, liquidation buyers, flippers, collectors.

**Problem:** buying inventory without a reliable max-buy decision traps cash.

**Offer:** evidence-backed identification, valuation, buy ceiling, and disposition plan.

**Price:** $299–$1,000+ operator stack test range.

**Delivery:** item/lot identification, comps/evidence, target buy, absolute max buy, exit path, operator receipt.

**Close condition:** a real purchase is made or rejected based on the output and the economic outcome is captured.

**Existing substrate:** FindSellProfit lineage, merchant economics, resale estate, evidence/valuation architecture.

# Cross-product architecture rules

The same substrate should power multiple products: evidence refs, identity resolution, signal fusion, bounded recipes, explicit unknowns, authorization, receipts, and outcome capture.

A product-specific UI is allowed. A product-specific duplicate backend is not.

Every paid service must create at least one reusable asset: a contract, test case, recipe, source adapter, evidence shape, delivery renderer, or outcome record.

# Launch order

1. Close one Agent Integrity pilot OR one AI Estate Forensic audit to create immediate high-ticket evidence about the harness market.
2. Close one $100 Property Opportunity Scan and immediately upsell the $499 Dossier.
3. Capture both end-to-end receipts.
4. Use the same receipt/evidence substrate to sell Workflow Replacement and Data Rescue.
5. Run one narrow Business Acquisition screen batch.
6. Use ProofOS internally for the highest-value transformation opportunities.
7. Convert the winning intelligence workflow into Managed Opportunity Desk recurring revenue.
8. Productize FindSellProfit after the first economic outcome.

# Stop conditions

Stop adding features when the buyer can buy, submit, receive, and act on the result.

Stop calling a candidate “live” until payment/quote, intake, fulfillment, delivery, receipt, and outcome have all been observed.

Stop creating new frameworks when an existing harness or contract can do the job.
