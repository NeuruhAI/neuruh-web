Boundaries

What Neuruh refuses to fake.

Our product is the credibility of a refusal. One unsupported claim would teach you to re-verify every honest number on this site — so the boundaries are published, and they bind this website too.

01

No fake live status.

If a number is a dated snapshot, it says so on the surface that shows it. Nothing on this site pretends to be a live feed — live connectivity is NONE and labeled.

02

No missing data turned into zero.

An unknown stays UNKNOWN. The truth compiler fails closed on unclassified numbers rather than guessing.

03

No AI output becomes authority by itself.

A model can propose. Only a checked authority gate can permit — approvals are scoped and one-shot, and a refusal is receipted with the same care as an action.

04

No real-money claim without a real transaction.

Demo economics are labeled synthetic inside the products and here. The Liquidity Engine’s closes say "no real money moved" on every row.

05

No production claim without a deployed, attested release.

DeedSonar may say "in production" because its health endpoint attests its own SHA. Nothing else on this site carries that label.

06

No fabricated case studies, customers, or partners.

The site shows sample-labeled and synthetic-labeled product surfaces — never invented logos or testimonials.
