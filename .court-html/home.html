Boundaries

What Neuruh refuses to fake.

01

No fake live status.

If a number is a dated snapshot, it says so. Nothing on this site pretends to be a live feed.

02

No AI output becomes authority by itself.

A model can propose. Only a checked authority gate can permit — and refusals are receipted like actions.

03

No real-money claim without a real transaction.

Demo economics are labeled synthetic in the product and on this site. Revenue to date: $0 — stated plainly.
