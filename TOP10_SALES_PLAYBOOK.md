## Where to sell

### Agent Integrity

**Best channels:** direct founder outreach, AI agencies, agent platform integrators, boutique consultancies, PE operating partners, technical founders posting about agents in production.

**Target titles:** CTO, Head of AI, VP Engineering, AI Product Lead, Automation Lead, CIO in smaller organizations.

**Trigger events:** agent moved from demo to production, customer acceptance requirement, regulated workflow, repeated agent regressions, enterprise procurement, AI incident, new agent deployment program.

**Pitch:** “You do not need another model. You need proof that the agent you ship today is still the agent you authorized yesterday.”

**Close:** one workflow, one baseline, one receipt.

### AI Estate Forensic

**Best channels:** founder communities, PE diligence, fractional CTOs, AI transformation consultants, agencies inheriting bad automation, acquisition integration work.

**Target titles:** Founder/CEO, CTO, operating partner, technical diligence lead.

**Trigger events:** stalled build, agency transition, “we have three AI systems and nobody knows which is canonical,” acquisition, team turnover, cost blowout.

**Pitch:** “Before you spend another dollar on the AI stack, find out what you already own.”

### Property Opportunity Scan / Dossier

**Best channels:** land-investor communities, investor Facebook groups, RE newsletters, local brokers, land wholesalers, county-focused prospecting, direct referral.

**Target titles:** land investor, acquisitions manager, developer, broker, small-family-office analyst.

**Trigger events:** property under contract, unknown owner, hard-to-underwrite rural parcel, flood concern, estate property, absentee owner.

**Pitch:** “Give us the parcel. We will tell you whether it deserves more of your time before you spend more money on it.”

### Business Acquisition Intelligence

**Best channels:** search funds, SMB acquisition communities, broker relationships, independent sponsors, family-office networks, investor newsletters.

**Target titles:** searcher, independent sponsor, acquisition entrepreneur, business broker, investor.

**Trigger events:** target vertical selected, LOI pipeline thin, broker list overloaded, succession/retirement thesis, real estate attached to the operating business.

**Pitch:** “We do not hand you another list of businesses. We expose the few that deserve diligence and explain why.”

### Data Rescue

**Best channels:** local SMBs, CRM consultants, operations consultants, data migration projects, Upwork-style marketplaces, direct outreach to operations managers.

**Target titles:** COO, operations manager, sales ops, admin lead, CRM consultant.

**Pitch:** “Send the spreadsheet everyone is afraid to edit.”

### Workflow Replacement Sprint

**Best channels:** local contractors, field-service operators, agencies, professional services, referrals, vertical associations.

**Target titles:** owner, GM, operations manager, service manager.

**Pitch:** “Pick the one workflow that wastes the most labor every week. We replace that one first.”

### ProofOS / Future-State Twin

**Best channels:** PE operating partners, consultants, transformation executives, technical due diligence, enterprise innovation groups.

**Pitch:** “We turn the transformation story into an evidence-backed current-state and future-state model your buyer can inspect.”

### Managed Opportunity Desk

Sell only after a one-off intelligence product creates a real customer outcome.

**Best channels:** existing buyers of scans/dossiers, investors with repeat search needs, niche acquisition buyers.

**Pitch:** “You already paid us to find one opportunity. Keep the desk running against your buy box.”

### FindSellProfit

**Best channels:** local resellers, liquidation businesses, estate cleanout operators, marketplace sellers, collectibles dealers.

**Pitch:** “Before you buy the lot, know your ceiling and your exit.”

## Sales sequence

1. Pick a buyer whose decision is expensive.
2. Show one result, not the architecture.
3. Sell a fixed-scope pilot.
4. Take payment or signed authorization before expanding scope.
5. Deliver the artifact and receipt.
6. Ask what decision changed.
7. Convert the changed decision into the upsell.

## Offer ladder

**Entry:** $100 scan / $299 Data Rescue / $750 business screen.

**Core:** $499 dossier / $1,250 workflow sprint / $2,500 forensic or ProofOS diagnostic.

**High-ticket:** $7,500+ transformation / implementation / acquisition mandate.

**Recurring:** $1,500–$5,000/mo managed desk or assurance.

## Objection handling

**“We already have ChatGPT/Claude.”**

“That answers questions. This product proves whether the workflow can be trusted and what happened after the answer.”

**“We have internal engineers.”**

“Good. This is the acceptance and evidence layer, so your engineers can ship without being their own compliance department.”

**“Can you automate all of this?”**

“Not on day one. We automate the bounded slice that can be proven, then expand from outcomes.”

**“Why not just buy software?”**

“Because the first problem is usually uncertainty about what should be bought or automated. We establish the decision boundary first.”
