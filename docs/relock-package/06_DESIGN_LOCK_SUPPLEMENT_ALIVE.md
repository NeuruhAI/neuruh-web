# Direction A Design Lock Supplement — ALIVE

This supplement does not reopen the approved Biological-Architectural art direction.
It adds semantic, state, data, and truth constraints required for production.

## Locked additions

1. The N-Cell is a stateful representation of the Neuruh organism.
2. Every visible biological event maps to a named system event or state.
3. Ambient motion is restrained and never implies execution, learning, or health.
4. Live, snapshot, sandbox, conceptual, planned, blocked, and unknown are visually and
   textually distinct.
5. The public experience is read-only.
6. The public truth feed is allowlisted, timestamped, source-bearing, and deterministic.
7. Galaxy nodes and edges are data-defined, not hand-authored visual fiction.
8. Edge type, direction, health, and criticality are revealed on selection.
9. Node size has one explicit meaning per lens.
10. Actual history, forecast, and scenario are separate time bands.
11. Contradiction is a first-class visual object.
12. Every compressed view expands to evidence.
13. Reduced-motion and tier-zero retain all truth semantics.
14. Product worlds retain independent visual languages.
15. Neuruh.com may be cinematic; the proof layer must remain inspectable.
16. No private archive, personal memory, customer data, secret, or sensitive system detail
    is exposed.
17. No seeded fixture may present as current operational truth.
18. No visual quality requirement may override claim discipline.

## Production acceptance

The flagship fails semantic acceptance when:

- removing the 3D layer leaves only generic marketing copy;
- motion cannot explain which Neuruh system changed;
- product cards could be replaced by any AI company's cards;
- a "live" number lacks timestamp and source;
- a green state lacks evidence;
- a contradiction is hidden;
- the Galaxy is illustrative rather than manifest-driven;
- the N-Cell is beautiful but operationally meaningless.
