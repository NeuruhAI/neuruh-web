Every chapter is a record in `packages/public-truth/experience.ts`. Essential meaning lives in
semantic DOM; the scene consumes only deterministic state/camera bindings.

| Chapter | Neuruh system | Route | Truth boundary |
|---|---|---|---|
| Presence | Public self-model | `/` | Verified snapshot; no live connector |
| The Estate | Archive Refinery / Local AI Estate | `/organism#archive-refinery` | No private inventory or remembered count |

truth labels. Galaxy’s 2D foundation uses lists and sourced edges without a canvas dependency.
