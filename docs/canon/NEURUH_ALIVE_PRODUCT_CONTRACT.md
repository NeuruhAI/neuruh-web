# Neuruh Alive Product Contract

Status: canonical semantic contract for P1, dated 2026-08-01.

## Product ruling

Neuruh.com is the read-only, publicly safe operating projection of a modulated
agentic organization. It shows how independent worlds, organs, memory systems,
evidence systems, governance, and execution contracts become one organism without
becoming one monolithic application.

The product must continuously answer:

1. What exists?
2. What state is it in?
3. What does it depend on?
4. What evidence supports that state?
5. Who or what owns the next decision?
6. What should happen next?
7. What changed over time?
8. Is the claim live, a verified snapshot, sandbox, conceptual, planned, blocked, or unknown?

## Definition of alive

A public surface is alive only when it renders a verified timestamped snapshot,
reacts to an evidenced event, exposes an evidence or decision chain, permits traversal
of sourced relationships, changes through the governed state machine, or truthfully
labels itself sandbox/conceptual. Ambient motion is not life.

P1 is alive only in the verified-snapshot sense. It has no live connector, private
integration, external write, or production command authority.

## Public projection

The projection includes the Archive Refinery / Local AI Estate, Jeramie Neural Core,
Cockpit / Galaxy, Adaptive Intelligence, Founder Command, Execution Platform, product
worlds, commercialization routes, founder architecture, research boundary, evidence,
contradictions, receipts, and technical proof gates.

It excludes private paths, archive contents, personal memory, customer data, secrets,
credentials, defense details, unreviewed claims, and unapproved future claims.

## Claim law

- Current repository, test, benchmark, deployment, provider, transaction, contract, and
  signed-receipt evidence outranks prose.
- Later founder corrections outrank earlier assistant framing.
- Architecture is not runtime proof; observed interfaces are not backend proof.
- Truth mode, maturity, evidence status, and living state are separate fields.
- Every public number is a classified metric with unit, timestamp, and source.
- Contradictory sources remain visible until a named authority resolves them.
- No visual state may promote snapshot, sandbox, conceptual, or planned data to LIVE.

## Read-only governance

The website may explain a sandbox decision/receipt trace but may not write to private or
production systems. Consequential actions remain human-gated. A refusal or blocked state
is a valid outcome and must never be restyled as success.

## Acceptance

P1 passes when the compiler fails closed, outputs are deterministic, routes use canonical
Neuruh terms and sourced relationships, tier-zero/reduced-motion routes retain truth
parity, and the existing P0 boundary/budget gates remain green. P1 does not certify G1 or G2.
